About us

We're making finding home feel like meeting a friend

roommates+ is built by S4S — a small team that got tired of watching students gamble on strangers from Facebook groups.

Where you live shapes everything — your sleep, your grades, your sanity. But who you live with shapes it more. Our mission is simple: nobody should have to choose a roommate from a blurry group-chat screenshot ever again.

What we believe

Four things we refuse to compromise on

🎓

Students first

Built around student budgets, student schedules, and September move-ins. Everything else is secondary.

🛡️

Safety is non-negotiable

Verified profiles and on-demand background checks. Meeting a stranger should never feel like a leap of faith.

⚡

Speed matters

Semesters don't wait. From download to "wanna view it together?" should take days, not months.

✨

Vibes are real data

Night owl vs early bird is a compatibility signal, not small talk. We treat lifestyle like the dealbreaker it is.

The story so far

From group-chat chaos to roommates+

The problem

The Facebook-groups era

We watched friends pick roommates from "ROOM AVAILABLE!! DM ME" posts — and spend the rest of the year regretting it. Scams, ghosting, and zero ways to vet anyone.

The spark

First prototype

What if finding a roommate felt like a dating app — swipe, match, chat — but for the most important platonic relationship of your twenties? We built it in a semester.

Launch

roommates+ goes live

Swipe-based discovery, real-time chat, dual seeker/owner modes, and filters deep enough to exclude cat allergies. Students started matching on day one.

Today

Premium & AI matching

AI compatibility scoring, on-demand background checks, and the feature we're proudest of: meeting the people who want the same unit as you, before anyone signs.
